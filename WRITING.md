# Internal writing

Lead with the answer, outcome, or required action. Use direct sentences that read naturally aloud. Preserve exact commands, values, facts, limits, and uncertainty.

- Prefer concrete verbs and stable technical terms.
- Remove throat-clearing, performed enthusiasm, filler intensifiers, rhetorical questions, and decorative conclusions.
- Keep one required action in each procedural step.
- Name the command, file, count, state, or failure when it matters.
- Report skipped checks and uncertainty once, beside the claim they limit.
- Use bullets for parallel facts and prose for connected reasoning.
- Do not use an em dash in authored prose.
- Stop when the useful content ends.
