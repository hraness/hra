- `hra-v1.md` is the active product, implementation, verification, migration, and release plan.
- `hra-v2.md` is the proposed provider-neutral, swarm-coordination, web-surface, and documentation plan built from the 2026-09-02 audits. It is not adopted until its open questions are closed.

# Guidelines

- Keep one current status for each phase and name the evidence needed to close it.
- Record design changes where they alter authority, recovery, privacy, compatibility, or public claims.
- Separate completed evidence from intended work. Do not mark a requirement complete from a mock when it requires live provider or platform proof.
